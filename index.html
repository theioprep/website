The problem

English mark schemes reward a precision that's rarely taught directly.

Most students can write in complete sentences — very few are shown exactly what an examiner is scoring against, for Paper 1's unseen commentary or Paper 2's comparative essay. Good tutoring fixes this, but it is expensive and inconsistent, and a single one-to-one session can cost more than a month of structured lessons. Add the Individual Oral and the Extended Essay on top, and most students are left to work out how it all connects on their own.
